Delete your Everslot account

Everslot by [DEVELOPER NAME]. You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by e-mail.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Everslot and go to Settings › Account › Delete account.
  2. Read what will be deleted. If you want a copy, export your data first (Settings › Sync & data).
  3. Confirm your identity (for example with a new e-mail code).
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and data are deleted from our servers right away, and the app removes the data stored on that device.

Option 2 — by e-mail

  1. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject “Delete my Everslot account”. If you use Sign in with Apple with a hidden address, write from the address that receives those relayed e-mails.
  2. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address, name and linked sign-in methods.
  • Everything you created: tasks, lists and items, habits and check-ins, quit trackers, goals, notes, categories, tags, reminder rules, settings and inbox notifications.
  • All attached photos and files.
  • Your registered devices, push tokens and scheduled notifications.

When

  • Your account and content are deleted immediately when the deletion completes, and attached files at the same time.
  • Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Crash reports are not linked to your account and expire after 90 days.
  • We keep only what the law requires, such as the e-mails of a deletion request handled by e-mail.

On your devices

The device you delete from is wiped. On your other devices, sign out or remove the app to delete the copy stored there. Guest accounts can be deleted the same way in the app; a guest account not used for 90 days may also be deleted automatically. In local-only mode your data exists only on your device: removing the app deletes it.

Delete some data only

You do not have to delete your account to remove data: delete any item in the app, then use Delete forever in the Trash to erase it right away. Otherwise, deleted items are erased from our servers 90 days after deletion.
