Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Use a one-time code sent to your e-mail address, Google or Apple. You can also tap Continue without account to start as a guest and add an e-mail address, Google or Apple later — your data stays in the same account.

Does Everslot work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on your device first. Changes sync automatically when you are back online.

How do I use Everslot on several devices?

Sign in with the same account on each device. Your data syncs between them, and the sync status and your devices are listed in Settings › Sync & data.

My reminders are late or missing. What can I check?
  • Allow notifications for Everslot in your device settings and check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not silencing them.
  • Android: allow Alarms & reminders for exact timing, and exclude Everslot from battery optimisation if your phone stops apps in the background.
  • iPhone and iPad: open Everslot from time to time — the system limits how many reminders an app can schedule ahead, and the app refreshes them when it runs.
  • Check quiet hours and mutes in Settings › Notifications.
Can I hide what my notifications say?

Yes. Turn on Hide content in notifications in Settings › Notifications: notifications then show a generic text, and the details stay in the in-app inbox.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings › Sync & data and export everything as JSON (complete, can be imported back) or as CSV files.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleted tasks, lists, items, habits and attachments stay in the Trash for 30 days (Settings › Sync & data) where you can restore them.

How do I change the week start, time format or time zone?

In Settings › Regional. You can also choose when your habit day starts, for example at 04:00.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on the app lock in Settings › Privacy & security to unlock with Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode.

Are quit-tracker health milestones medical advice?

No. They are general educational estimates based on public health sources. Talk to a healthcare professional about your health.

Which languages are available?

English, French and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout in Arabic.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings › Account › Delete account, or by e-mail — see how to delete your account.
