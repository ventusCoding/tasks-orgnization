Privacy policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

At a glance

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of your data and no analytics tools.
  • Your content is used only to run Everslot for you: sync, reminders and backups.
  • Insights and statistics are computed on your device.
  • Habit and quit-tracker data is never used for advertising and never shared.
  • You can export everything or delete your account at any time, in the app.

1. Who is responsible

Everslot is provided by [DEVELOPER NAME] (“we”, “us”), the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. This policy covers the Everslot apps for iOS and Android and this website.

2. Data we collect

Account

  • Your e-mail address when you sign in with an e-mail code, Google or Apple (with Apple you can hide your address behind a relay address).
  • Your name and profile photo if you add them, or the name Apple or Google share at sign-in.
  • An account identifier and the sign-in methods linked to your account.
  • Guest accounts (“Continue without account”) have no e-mail address.

Content you create

  • Tasks, schedules and repeat rules, time entries and notes.
  • Checklists and their items, statuses and reason notes.
  • Photos and files you attach (location metadata is removed from photos before upload).
  • Habits, check-ins, values, notes and mood; quit trackers with relapses, cravings (intensity, trigger, place, coping), consumption, costs and milestones; goals.
  • Categories, tags, saved views, reminder rules, settings, in-app inbox notifications, and a history of changes used for statistics and undo.

Device and technical data

  • A random device identifier created by the app, the device name and model, platform, operating system version, app version, language and time zone.
  • Your push notification token, whether notifications and exact alarms are allowed, how far ahead reminders are scheduled on the device, and when the app was last opened — so each reminder reaches the right device only once.
  • Technical server logs, which include IP addresses, kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

Crash reports

If crash reporting is on, a crash sends a report through Firebase Crashlytics: the error and stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, a crash-reporting installation identifier and short technical logs that never contain your content. You can turn crash reporting off in Settings › Privacy & security.

Support

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and what you send us.

3. Data we do not collect

  • No location: the app does not ask for location access (a place you type into a task is simply part of your content).
  • No contacts, no browsing history and no advertising identifier.
  • No product analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase is not used and analytics collection is disabled.
  • No data from Apple Health or Health Connect: this version does not connect to them.
  • Biometric data used for the optional app lock stays in your device’s secure hardware; we never receive it.

4. How we use data and why

  • To provide Everslot — your account, sync between your devices, reminders, push notifications, attachments and the in-app inbox. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure and reliable — abuse and fraud prevention, rate limiting, troubleshooting. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To fix crashes — only if crash reporting is on. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a stable app; you can object by turning it off.
  • To answer you — support requests and messages about your account, such as sign-in codes or deletion confirmations.
  • To comply with the law — when a legal obligation requires it.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Habits and quit trackers

Entries such as cigarettes or drinks logged, cravings, relapses or mood can reveal information about your health or lifestyle. We treat them as sensitive: they are processed only to provide the features you use, statistics about them are computed on your device, they are never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with anyone other than the providers that host the service for us. Where the law treats this data as health data, we process it on the basis of your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the entries or your account.

6. Service providers and international transfers

We use a few providers that process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Your account and synced content are stored in the [SUPABASE REGION] region.
  • Google Firebase — Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications and Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports.
  • Apple — the Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to Apple devices; Sign in with Apple if you choose it. Google Sign-In if you choose it.
  • An e-mail delivery provider that sends sign-in codes and account e-mails on our behalf.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, which uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Some providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

We never sell your personal data and never share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to a successor if Everslot is transferred — in that case this policy keeps applying to your data and we will tell you beforehand.

7. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device and, when another device needs to be reached, sent as push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on Apple devices, the Apple Push Notification service. Push notifications contain the reminder text (for example a task title) unless you turn on Hide content in notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data and content: as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted items: in the Trash for 30 days, then permanently erased from our servers 90 days after deletion, or immediately with Delete forever.
  • In-app inbox notifications: removed from your inbox after 90 days and erased from our servers 90 days later.
  • Notification delivery records: deleted after 14 to 30 days.
  • Push tokens: cleared after 120 days without using the app on that device, or when you sign out.
  • Guest accounts not used for 90 days may be deleted automatically, with their data.
  • Server logs: no longer than 28 days. Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Backups: overwritten within 30 days.
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.

When you delete your account, your data is erased as described on the account deletion page.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California law), you can:

  • access your data and get a copy — the app exports everything as JSON or CSV (Settings › Sync & data);
  • correct it — you can edit everything in the app;
  • delete it — delete items, or your whole account in Settings › Account › Delete account;
  • restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • receive your data in a portable format (the JSON export);
  • know what we collect and why, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share personal information and only use sensitive information to provide the service.

To exercise a right, use the app or write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the e-mail address of your account. We answer within one month (45 days where California law applies). You can also complain to your data protection authority.

10. Data on your device and local-only mode

Everslot stores your data on your device first, in the app’s private storage, so it works offline. If you use Everslot without cloud sync (local-only mode), your content stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. Removing the app deletes this data unless you exported it; your device’s own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup), if you use one, may include it under your platform account’s terms.

11. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest at our hosting provider.
  • Each account is isolated by database access rules; attached files are private and shared only through short-lived links.
  • Server secrets never ship in the app; your session is kept in the device’s secure storage.
  • Optional app lock with Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach when the law requires it.

12. Children

Everslot is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country to consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect their data; if you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date and tell you in the app before significant changes apply.
