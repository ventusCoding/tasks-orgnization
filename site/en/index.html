Everything your day needs, in one place

  • Plan

    A week table with 7 day columns and slots from 1 minute to 24 hours. Pinch to zoom, drag to move or resize, switch to the day list, month, agenda or a focus view of what’s now and next.

  • Repeat anything

    Fully free recurrence: every N minutes within a time window, several times a day, N times a week, after completion, with exceptions. Edit one occurrence, the following ones or the whole series.

  • Lists

    Card-style checklists with items nested as deep as you need, photos and files, and a status for every item — to do, ongoing, waiting, blocked, completed or cancelled — with a reason note.

  • Habits

    Yes/no, count, duration, numeric or “at most” goals on any schedule. Check in as done, partial, skipped or excused, backfill past days and add notes and mood.

  • Quit trackers

    A live clean-time counter, relapses and cravings with intensity and triggers, money and time saved, milestones, and a cut-down mode with a daily limit.

  • Insights

    Detailed statistics for every task, list, item and habit and for each section: streaks, habit strength, punctuality, planned versus actual time, time in each status, trends and records.

  • Reminders your way

    Several reminders per item, repeat-until-done nudges, quiet hours, snooze and actions from the notification, an in-app inbox and delivery to your other devices.

  • Today at a glance

    Your agenda, due habits, quit counters and pinned lists on one screen, plus quick add, global search and home-screen widgets.

  • Offline first

    Everything is saved on your device first and works without a connection. Sign in to sync across your phones and tablets.

Private by design

  • No ads, no tracking and no selling of your data.
  • Insights are computed on your device.
  • Habit and quit-tracker data is never used for advertising.
  • Export all your data or delete your account at any time, right in the app.
